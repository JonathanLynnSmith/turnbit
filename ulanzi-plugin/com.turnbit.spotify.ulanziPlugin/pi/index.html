turnbit navigation

Keep the turnbit desktop widget running.

Assign Navigation Wheel to the D100H dial. In lists, rotate to highlight an item; single-click to select it; double-click within 350 ms to go back one screen.

On Now Playing: rotate for volume, single-click Center to return to the song list, double-click Center or press Menu to go Home. The ten-second idle timeout shows Now Playing without changing these controls.

Top row, left to right: Previous Track, Play / Pause, Next Track. Tap Previous/Next to skip; hold for half a second to rewind/fast-forward continuously; release to stop seeking. Play first opens Now Playing from another screen without changing playback. On Now Playing, Play pauses/resumes.

Assign Menu / Back to a side button. It goes Home from Now Playing and back one level elsewhere.

Assign Like Current Song to toggle the playing song in or out of Liked Songs. Shuffle toggles on/off; Repeat cycles Off → All → One song → Off. These actions work from any page. Reconnect Spotify in turnbit to grant the new save permission. The remaining side buttons can also control volume or seeking.

The desktop widget connects to Spotify; no Spotify credentials are stored in this plugin.
