`spotify-logo-black.png` and `spotify-logo-white.png` are the unmodified Spotify
icon and wordmark from Spotify's official press assets:
https://newsroom.spotify.com/media-kit/logo-and-brand-assets/

Downloaded from:
https://storage.googleapis.com/pr-newsroom-wp/1/2023/05/Spotify_Full_Logo_RGB_Black.png
https://storage.googleapis.com/pr-newsroom-wp/1/2023/05/Spotify_Full_Logo_RGB_White.png

`spotify-like.svg` and `spotify-liked.svg` are Spotify's supplied save-control
states from https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/design.

Spotify's logos and icons remain the property of Spotify. The white logo links
back to Spotify when connected; the save icons show Liked Songs state.

`turnbit-mark.svg` is the unchanged symbol path from the project's existing
`assets/branding/turnbit-logo-white-tm.svg`. It identifies a missing cover; it is
not replacement album artwork. The same flat sleeve is used at all player sizes.
