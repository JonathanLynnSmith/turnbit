# Zune reference target

Use the **Zune 80, 2007**, consistently. Do not mix the Zune HD's oversized page
headings and category subtitles into this model's UI.

Photo references inspected:

- [iFixit Zune 80](https://www.ifixit.com/Device/Microsoft_Zune_80): blank pad,
  metallic rim, shallow oval side buttons, thin top bezel.
- [Futurelooks hands-on review](https://www.futurelooks.com/microsoft-zune-80gb-digital-media-player-review/2/):
  photos `zune80gb-4.jpg` and `-5.jpg` show the pad and case edges; `-8.jpg`
  shows the direct text home menu and blue geometric wallpaper; `-9.jpg` and
  `-11.jpg` show horizontal categories and blue list selection.
- [CESPage Zune 80](https://cespage.com/zune/zune80gb.html): front-view product
  image and 61.1 × 108.2 mm dimensions used for the body aspect ratio.
- [Real playback photo](https://i.ebayimg.com/images/g/37cAAeSwhbRpjOyM/s-l1200.webp):
  full-width square album cover, thin progress line, time labels, compact song,
  album, and artist text beneath. No large Now Playing heading or persistent
  volume slider.

The wallpaper is an original vector approximation, not a copied photo asset.
Reference photographs remain under artifacts and are not packaged in the app.

DialPod still browses Spotify playlists and Liked Songs. It does not fabricate
video, FM radio, social, marketplace, or local-library functions. Screen Light
mode and alternative case finishes are retained as optional customizations.
The D100H dial substitutes for touchpad scrolling; Center playback options,
Back, Back-hold Home, direct Play/Pause, and live hold-to-seek are supported.
